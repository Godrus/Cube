# M2-T task classification (proposed)

**Status:** **Proposed.** Classification of the 75 M2-T tasks of the previous graph (`docs/m2-delivery-review/M2_TASK_GRAPH.md`) plus two additional rows (`PERS-06` joins M2-T; `TGAP-09` is the M2 extension of it). Generator output for the dependency column; the classification text is the reviewer's. Classification is **not** a substitute for dependency analysis: the dependencies of every task are in `M2T_OPTIMIZED_TASK_GRAPH.md`.

**Definitions** (from the brief). MUST_HAVE: without the task the M2-T acceptance criteria (`M2T_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md`) cannot be met. SHOULD_HAVE: useful for quality or efficiency, not required for the first successful M2-T result. DEFERRED: not needed for M2-T. ARCHITECTURE_COMPATIBILITY_ONLY: not needed as an implementation, but a contract or boundary must exist.

## Counts (of the 75)

| Class | Tasks |
|---|---|
| MUST_HAVE | **67** |
| SHOULD_HAVE | 5 (`TEST-05`, `TEST-08`, `INT-01`, `API-05`, `GM-16`) |
| ARCHITECTURE_COMPATIBILITY_ONLY | 1 (`NET-01`) |
| DEFERRED (leave M2-T) | 2 (`NET-02`, `CLI-05`) |

The high MUST_HAVE share is expected: the previous graph was already a slice (the ADG has 147 tasks), and the review found few tasks in it that the acceptance criteria do not need. The reductions are mostly inside tasks (the "smallest acceptable implementation" column) and in dependencies, not in the number of tasks. SHOULD_HAVE tasks still run before M2-T acceptance where their evidence is cited (`TEST-05`, `TEST-08` for T-09/T-10; `API-05` as a CI check); they are off the critical path.
